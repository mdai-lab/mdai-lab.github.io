Postdoctoral and Research Scientist Positions

We're hiring! We are looking for a postdoc to join our team and help develop new sequencing and imaging technologies!

Specific directions:

  • 3D Spatial transcriptomics and AI tissue model (built on our recent Compression Sequencing method, enables scalable 3D tissue profiling)
  • Single-molecule protein sequencing and proteoform profiling (Super-resolution imaging of full-length proteins and PTMs)

 

This is an opportunity to take scientific ownership and make contribution in an interdisciplinary and translational project, learn and develop skills across biophysics, engineering and computational analysis, and help advance promising technologies toward diagnostic application and commercialization.

 

We look for candidates with an enthusiasm in method development and strengths in one or more of the following areas.

  • Molecular biology and NGS assay development
  • Single-cell and spatial transcriptomics
  • Hydrogel chemistry and tissue imaging
  • Single-molecule biophysics and super-resolution imaging

 

A PhD degree (or anticipating one) in related areas is required. An interest in biotechnology entrepreneurship is especially welcome.

Earliest start date: Dec 2026

For inquiries and applications, please email mingjie.dai@rice.edu.
